## Enbridge Line 5

- **operator**: Enbridge (Lakehead System)  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **product**: Synthetic crude, NGLs, sweet crude, light sour crude  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **diameter_in**: 30  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **capacity**: 540,000 bbl/d (as of 2013; up from 490,000)  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **length**: 645 mi  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **route**: Superior WI -> Upper Peninsula -> Rapid River -> Straits of Mackinac -> Lewiston -> around Saginaw Bay/Bay City -> Marysville MI -> under St. Clair River -> Sarnia ON  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **endpoints**: ['Superior, WI', 'Sarnia, ON']  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **in_service**: 1953  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **status**: Operating; Michigan seeking shutdown in court; Canada invoked 1977 transit treaty (2021, 2022); Wisconsin Bad River reroute litigation  [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5) [src](https://www.michbar.org/Portals/0/opinions/supreme/2026/073126/86270.pdf)
- **waypoints (approx)**: Superior 46.72,-92.1; RapidRiver 45.92,-86.96; Mackinaw_N 45.86,-84.77; McGulpin 45.79,-84.77; Lewiston 44.88,-84.31; BayCity 43.59,-83.89; Marysville 42.91,-82.49; Sarnia 42.97,-82.4
- **incident/controversy**: Spilled over 1.1 million gallons 1967-2017 [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **incident/controversy**: 2018 tugboat anchor strike damaged Straits segment [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **incident/controversy**: June 2020 east-leg screw anchor support shifted; east leg shut temporarily [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5)
- **incident/controversy**: Bad River Band case: court ordered Enbridge to reroute or shut down in Wisconsin by June 2026; 7th Circuit (July 30 2026) remanded to fashion injunction [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enbridge_Line_5) [src](https://www.michbar.org/Portals/0/opinions/supreme/2026/073126/86270.pdf)
